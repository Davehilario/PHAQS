Targeted queue display fix

- APT-xxxxx is shown only as an appointment reference before arrival.
- Before check-in, the patient sees Upcoming Appointment and Awaiting Check-in.
- People-ahead and estimated waiting time are hidden until a live queue entry exists.
- After QR or manual check-in, a live queue number is generated:
  A-001 for regular queue
  P-001 for verified priority queue
  E-001 for emergency walk-in
- No other modules were changed.
