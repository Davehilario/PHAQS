Patient

- Recommendation explanation opens beside the info button instead of at the bottom of the screen.
- My Appointments primary UI changed to the same dark navy blue used by PHAQS.
- Profile photo/profile JSON is preserved when saving another profile section.
- PhilHealth and Benefits Camera buttons no longer silently fall back to a file picker when camera permission fails; Upload remains a separate action.

Admin / RHU

- Announcement priority, target barangay, publish time, and expiration time are now saved in the database.
- Patient announcement visibility respects branch, barangay, publish time, and expiration time.
