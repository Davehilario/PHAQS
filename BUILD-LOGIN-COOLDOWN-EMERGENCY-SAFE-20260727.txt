LOGIN COOLDOWN

Patient: 5 failed attempts = 15-minute temporary cooldown.
Admin/RHU Staff: 5 failed attempts = 5-minute per-account cooldown.
Successful login resets the counter.
Unknown email and wrong password both show: Invalid email or password.

Emergency continuity:
- The cooldown affects only the specific account, not the whole system or RHU.
- Another authorized RHU staff account remains usable.
- Emergency walk-in handling belongs to RHU staff and does not require the administrator account.
